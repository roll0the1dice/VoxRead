EdgeTtsTokenV2.kt is a Kotlin adaptation of the v2 protocol digest in
edge-tts 7.3.0, src/edge_tts/drm.py, by the edge-tts contributors.
Source: https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts/tree/7.3.0

The adaptation is licensed under LGPL-3.0. The upstream license is in
LICENSE.txt; its incorporated GPL-3.0 terms are in GPL-3.txt.
VoxRead modifications: JVM 32-bit arithmetic, a compact SHA-256 round
loop, and integration with the existing Android clock-skew correction.
The rest of the Readium toolkit retains its existing license.
